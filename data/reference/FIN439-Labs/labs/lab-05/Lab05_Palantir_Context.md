## Current Research Position

The strongest part of the current Palantir packet is the operating story. The evidence suggests that Palantir's AIP and commercial-growth narrative is showing measurable traction, especially in U.S. commercial revenue and contract value.

The weakest part is valuation. The research has not yet answered whether the market price already reflects too much of the growth story. The packet also needs more independent risk evidence before the investment call can move beyond watch-defer.

Current working call: **watch-defer**  
Reason: keep researching, but do not initiate a buy from the existing evidence packet alone.

## Falsification Question

The key falsification question from the earlier labs is:

> What evidence would most strongly show that my current thesis on Palantir is wrong, and where would that evidence appear in the filing or operating disclosures?

For Lab 05, the most useful negative evidence would be:

- U.S. commercial growth slowing sharply
- AIP demand failing to convert into durable contracts
- Margins weakening as growth scales
- Customer concentration or government-contract risk becoming more important than expected
- Valuation multiples or a DCF implying that the current price already assumes unrealistic growth

## What Lab 05 Should Build Next

Lab 05 should probably focus on converting the research packet into a valuation argument.

Useful next steps:

1. Add a sourced valuation multiple comparison against relevant software / AI infrastructure peers.
2. Build a simple DCF or scenario table with base, upside, and downside assumptions.
3. Tie each scenario to observable evidence: revenue growth, margin trajectory, free cash flow, and share dilution.
4. Add at least one independent risk source that challenges management's framing.
5. Decide what evidence would be strong enough to change the call from watch-defer to initiate, or from watch-defer to do not initiate.
