## How the analysis evolved

Earlier work applied growth directly to free cash flow and produced a historical estimate of $158.16 per share. A later integrated forecast linked operating drivers to financial statements and produced $34.53 per share. The October 6 independent snapshot produced $29.76 per share, with an approximate $16-$65 causal DCF range. These are different model specifications and historical stages, not Edition A price targets or values to average.

After code corrections, the current model has a $30.12 base value and a $16.66-$65.91 range per diluted economic share. The valuation cutoff is October 5, 2026, and the reference market price is $189.40. The current model's price-action conclusion is do not initiate at that price. Both the earlier integrated model and the current model reject an immediate purchase under their tested assumptions.

## Evidence and model checks

The analysis now uses a linked operating forecast and FCFF valuation, with explicit source and period boundaries. It distinguishes balance-sheet cash from restricted cash, assesses marketable securities separately, treats compensation as an economic cost, considers customer prepayment funding, and checks forecast and terminal reinvestment consistency. The October 5 valuation cutoff is distinct from the change in method and evidence since Edition A.

Several AI-assisted interpretations were qualified through source and definition checks. Restricted cash is not automatically available to shareholders. Net operating loss tax assets are distinguished from pretax loss pools. Weighted-average EPS shares are distinguished from dated economic shares and awards. Comparative EPS corrections are disclosed rather than used to silently alter the baseline.

An earlier partner challenge questioned whether the growth sensitivity range was wider than the margin range. Equal-proportional tests show that margin has a larger spread in 2030 operating profit and free cash flow, while growth has a larger spread in value per share because it changes the terminal revenue base. The ranking depends on the output, tested range, and assumptions held fixed.
